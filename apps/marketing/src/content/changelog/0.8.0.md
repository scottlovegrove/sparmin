---
version: 0.8.0
summary: A session that couldn't reach your phone as you finished now sends itself as soon as it can, instead of waiting for you to open the app again.
---

- **Your visit arrives on its own.** Ending a session in a wet changing room
  often meant your watch had no line to your phone at that exact moment, so the
  visit sat on your wrist until the next time you happened to open Sparmin —
  sometimes hours later. It now keeps trying quietly in the background and
  arrives within a few minutes of your phone coming back into reach.
- **Only when something is waiting.** The watch checks nothing, and wakes for
  nothing, when everything has already been sent.
- **A session the companion refuses is no longer retried for ever.** It is
  dropped, leaving the ones behind it free to go. Nothing is lost either way:
  every visit is still saved to Garmin Connect, and can be imported from there.
- Sparmin now asks for the background permission when you install or update it.
  That is what lets the watch finish sending after you have put it down.
