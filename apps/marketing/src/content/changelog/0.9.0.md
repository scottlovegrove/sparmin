---
version: 0.9.0
summary: When something goes wrong on your wrist, the watch now keeps a proper record of it and sends it to your Sparmin account, so it can be looked at afterwards instead of guessed at.
---

- **The watch keeps a dated record of what it was doing.** Its diagnostic log
  used to show a time of day and nothing else, which made two entries a
  fortnight apart look identical. Every line now carries its date, and a live
  session leaves a mark every few minutes, so the account of a visit that ended
  unexpectedly no longer stops at whatever you last pressed.
- **That record goes to your Sparmin account.** Next time your watch has your
  phone, it sends the log to the companion, where it can be read on a real
  screen. It is diagnostics only — what the app did and when, never anything
  about you beyond the visits you were already sending.
- **It stays with the account it was written for.** Unlinking your watch, or the
  companion no longer recognising it, stops anything already recorded from being
  sent. Those lines stay readable on the watch under Settings → Diagnostics.
- Nothing about recording a visit has changed, and the log never delays a
  session on its way to the companion.
