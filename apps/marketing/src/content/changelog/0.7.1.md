---
version: 0.7.1
summary: Linking no longer gives up the moment a single request to your phone fails.
---

- **Linking survives a brief hiccup.** The watch checks every few seconds whether
  you've entered its code, and one failed check — a phone in a locker, a moment
  out of range — was enough to abandon the whole attempt and ask you to start
  again, even though the code on screen was still perfectly good. It now keeps
  trying, and only gives up when nothing has worked for a while.
