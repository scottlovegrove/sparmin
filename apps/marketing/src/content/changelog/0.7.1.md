---
version: 0.7.1
summary: Settings is now a tile at the end of the activity strip on button watches, so you no longer have to know a hidden button hold to reach it.
---

- **Settings is reachable on button watches.** The idle screen showed a Settings
  button that only a touchscreen could press; on a Forerunner the only way in
  was holding Up, which nothing told you about. Cycle past the last activity
  with Up or Down and Settings is now the next tile along, opened with Start
  like any other.
- Holding Up still works where the watch offers it.
- The tile only appears before a session starts, which is the only time Settings
  will open — it edits the very activity list you'd be recording against.
