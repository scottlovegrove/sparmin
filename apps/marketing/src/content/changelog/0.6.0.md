---
version: 0.6.0
summary: Link the watch to your Sparmin account, and see what the app has been doing when something goes wrong.
---

- **You can now link this watch to your Sparmin account.** Settings → Link
  account shows a short code; type it into Sparmin in a browser and the watch is
  linked. Nothing has to be typed on the watch itself. Once it's linked, Settings
  shows **Account (linked)** rather than the invitation.
- **A new Diagnostics screen** under Settings shows what the app has recently
  been doing — asking for a code, waiting, linking, and the like. If something
  goes wrong when there's nobody around to watch it happen, that's where the
  answer is.
- Linking on its own doesn't send anything yet. Sending each session as you
  finish it comes next; for now, exporting the activity and importing it into
  Sparmin works exactly as before.
