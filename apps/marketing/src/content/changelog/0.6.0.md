---
version: 0.6.0
summary: Link the watch to your Sparmin account and your sessions send themselves — no exporting, no dragging files into a browser.
---

- **Your sessions now reach Sparmin on their own.** Link the watch once and every
  visit sends itself as you finish it. No export, no file to drag into a browser.
- **Linking is done from the watch.** Settings → Link account shows a short code;
  type it into Sparmin in a browser and you're linked. Nothing has to be typed on
  the watch. Afterwards Settings reads **Account (linked)**.
- **Sessions recorded away from your phone are kept and sent later**, next time
  you open the app with your phone nearby. Up to twenty are held; beyond that the
  oldest are dropped, and those are still in Garmin Connect to export by hand.
- **If you remove the watch from your account**, it notices the next time it
  tries to send, forgets the account and stops trying. Settings offers linking
  again.
- **Sending never touches the recording.** The activity is saved to the watch
  first and always — if a send fails, the visit is still on your watch and still
  in Garmin Connect, and importing the export works exactly as before.
- **A new Diagnostics screen** under Settings shows what the app has recently
  been doing. If something goes wrong when there's nobody around to watch it
  happen, that's where the answer is.
