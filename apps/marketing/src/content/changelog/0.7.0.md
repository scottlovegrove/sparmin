---
version: 0.7.0
summary: The watch now tells you which Sparmin account it is linked to, and lets you unlink without reaching for a browser.
---

- **Settings now shows the account your watch sends to.** It knew it was linked;
  it couldn't tell you to what. It shows the email address and the date it was
  linked, so you can check where your sessions are going without opening the
  companion.
- **You can unlink from the watch.** "Forget this account" stops it sending, and
  asks first. Anything still waiting to send is dropped with it — those sessions
  belong to the account you're leaving, and they're all still in Garmin Connect
  to import by hand.
- **"Link a different account" is now a deliberate choice.** Opening the account
  entry used to start a fresh pairing attempt straight away; now it shows you
  where you stand and lets you decide.
- A watch linked before this release will say it's linked but won't know the
  address. Linking again fills it in.
