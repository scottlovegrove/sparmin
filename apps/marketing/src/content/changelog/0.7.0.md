---
version: 0.7.0
summary: Sessions now send themselves to Sparmin when you finish, with no export needed.
---

- **Your sessions now reach Sparmin on their own.** Finish a visit and the watch
  sends it, so there's nothing to export and nothing to drag into a browser. It
  needs a linked account (Settings → Link account) and your phone in range.
- **Sessions recorded away from your phone are kept and sent later**, next time
  you open the app with your phone nearby. Up to twenty are held; beyond that the
  oldest are dropped, and those are still in Garmin Connect to export by hand.
- **If the link is removed**, the watch notices the next time it tries to send,
  forgets its account and stops trying. Settings shows **Link account** again.
- Sending never touches the recording. The activity is saved to the watch first
  and always — if a send fails, the visit is still on your watch and still in
  Garmin Connect, and importing the export works exactly as before.
