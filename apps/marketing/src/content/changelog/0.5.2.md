---
version: 0.5.2
summary: Long visits no longer crash the app when you save them, and the session summary keeps its last few characters.
---

- **Fixed a crash when saving a long visit.** On a session with enough stations
  in it — roughly seven or more — the app closed as you pressed Save, leaving the
  Connect IQ logo on screen. The activity was still recorded, but the last lap
  lost its station name and the whole-session summary never made it onto the
  activity. Visits that long now save normally.
- **The session summary is no longer clipped.** The one-line summary attached to
  the saved activity was losing a handful of characters off the end, so the
  transition total came out cut short or missing entirely. It now arrives whole.
- **Summary separators are now dashes** rather than dots — the same information,
  in a form that survives the trip into the activity file intact.
