---
version: 0.9.1
summary: The record the watch keeps of a visit now says what a shutdown interrupted, so a session that ends by itself explains itself instead of stopping mid-sentence.
---

- **A shutdown says what it took with it.** When something closes the app — the
  watch, or you — the log now records whether a session was live at the time,
  how far into it you were, and whether the recording had already begun saving.
  Until now it said only that the app had stopped, which is the least useful
  half of the answer.
- **The regular mark a live session leaves now includes the battery.** A watch
  closing an app to save power looks, from inside the app, exactly like any
  other shutdown. The battery level beside each mark is what tells the two
  apart afterwards.
- Diagnostics only. Nothing about recording a visit, or sending it to the
  companion, has changed.
